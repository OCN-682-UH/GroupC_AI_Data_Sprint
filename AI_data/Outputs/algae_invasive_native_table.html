a Percentage of 25-point quadrats in which the taxon was recorded (sampling effort in each header). Sites sampled differ between periods: 2024–2026 data come from 4 sites on Oahu only, so differences between periods partly reflect where sampling took place.
b Mean percentage of the 25 points covered by the taxon in quadrats where it was present. Older datasheets did not always record bare substrate, so cover is less comparable between periods than detection.
c Includes common genera such as Padina, Sargassum and Laurencia; genera with both native and introduced species in Hawaiʻi (e.g. Hypnea, Gracilaria) cannot be assigned an origin.
d Listed as introduced, invasive algae in Hawaiʻi (NOAA Hawaiʻi Integrated Ecosystem Assessment; U.S. Army Corps of Engineers). Avrainvillea lacerata includes records under its former name, A. amadelpha.
e Origin status from the team's algae origin lookup (Data/Algae). 'Native' statuses are provisional pending verification against a Hawaiian marine algae checklist. Only taxa identified to species are classed as native or invasive.
Data: OPIHI (Our Project In Hawaiʻi’s Intertidal) monitoring data, cleaned master dataset with team corrections (opihi_master_completed.csv); quadrat-level point-intercept records only (7,182 quadrats at 42 sites). Classroom group totals and cyanobacteria excluded. A quadrat counts once per row even if several taxa in that row were recorded in it. Exploratory summary; no statistical tests. Full per-taxon results: Outputs/algae_origin_summary.csv.
